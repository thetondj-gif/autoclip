# AutoClip licence and rights disposition

## Repository licence

The fork declares the AutoClip source under the MIT licence. Any retained or redistributed source must preserve the applicable copyright and licence notices.

## Separate rights requiring review

The software licence does not grant rights to source media, music, logos, likenesses, transcripts, downloaded platform content, third-party models or model outputs. Bundled fonts and optional diarisation/model dependencies retain their own terms.

## First-canary restrictions

- operator-owned or explicitly licensed local media only;
- no remote download;
- no DRM, paywall or platform-access workaround;
- no customer or confidential recording;
- no publication or commercial delivery;
- no gated diarisation model during the first acceptance;
- generated clips remain review evidence, not approved assets.

## Distribution gate

Before client or commercial use, record the source-media rights, music and font rights, model/provider terms, required attribution, retention period and publication approval for each job.
